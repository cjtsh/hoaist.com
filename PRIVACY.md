# HOAist Privacy Notice

**Effective date:** October 21, 2026

This notice describes the public HOAist website and demonstration experience.
It is not a promise about a future production service.

## Information handled

The static website may expose standard technical information processed by its
hosting, delivery, security, and domain providers, such as IP address,
request time, browser information, and requested files. The site links to
Google Fonts, GitHub-hosted assets, WhatsApp, and other third-party services;
those services may process requests under their own policies.

If you use the WhatsApp demo, WhatsApp and Meta may process your phone number,
messages, attachments, and related metadata. Bitseeker LLC may receive and
review messages sent to the demo to operate, test, and improve it. Do not send
passwords, financial information, health information, private HOA records,
or other sensitive information.

## Cookies and analytics

The public repository does not intentionally add a user account system or a
contact form. Hosting or linked services may use their own cookies, logs, or
analytics. Browser settings and those providers' policies control some of
that processing.

## Retention and deletion

Retention can vary by hosting and messaging providers. To request deletion of
information sent to the demo, use the contact method published on the website
and identify the conversation or phone number involved. We may need to retain
limited records where required for security, fraud prevention, or legal
compliance.

## Children

The demo is not directed to children under 13. Do not submit a child's
personal information.

## Changes and contact

This notice may be updated when the website or demo changes. If these
practices do not fit your use case, do not use the demo.
