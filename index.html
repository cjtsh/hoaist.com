A form files a question. An agent answers it.

Most HOA tools give residents a form, then a volunteer has to reply later. HOAist starts with the answer.

With a form

  1. Hunt for the link or the right email
  2. Pick a category and type it all out
  3. Wait for a volunteer to look it up
  4. Get a reply days later, maybe

With your HOAist agent

  1. Ask in WhatsApp, the way you'd text a neighbor
  2. Get answers with the page number
  3. If it's a problem, it's filed and routed for you
  4. Hear back in the same chat when it's handled

If you can send a WhatsApp, you can ask your agent.

An agent your community names

Your agent isn't a generic help desk. Your board picks its name and personality and puts your logo on it. It has a dedicated WhatsApp number, so residents save one contact for everything HOA.

  • Name: anything that fits your community.
  • Persona: warm and chatty, or short and to the point.
  • Look: your community's logo as the profile picture.
  • Knowledge: your governing documents, ARC guidelines, and board-confirmed answers.

We set it up with you. No volunteer has to configure anything.

Answers that get smarter, with the board's OK

When a resident asks something your documents don't cover, the agent doesn't guess. It asks the board. Once a board member confirms the answer, the agent remembers it for the next resident who asks.

Your agent only repeats what your documents say or what your board has confirmed.

Built for Florida HOAs

HOAist started in Florida and knows the questions Florida communities get.

Florida rules alongside yours

Your agent combines your documents with Florida statutes (Chapter 720 for HOAs, 718 for condos) and county rules, and says which one it's quoting.

Gated communities

Guest access, gate procedures, tailgating reports, and vehicle rules.

Storm season

"When can shutters go up?" and "Where do I put yard debris?" get answered from your own rules.

Seasonal residents

Snowbirds and renters get the same answers from anywhere, in an app they already use.
