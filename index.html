How it works

Nothing to install, no passwords, no forms. If residents can send a text, they can use HOAist.

  1. Your community gets its own agent

    A named agent with your logo and its own WhatsApp number, loaded with your governing documents. Pre-built for HOAs, and we set it up.

  2. Residents just text

    Ask "Can I paint my front door?" or report a broken light, gate tailgating, or a lost pet, with a photo. Answers come instantly, with sources.

  3. The right people handle it

    Reports become tickets routed to the right committee. Residents get updates in the same chat, and board members can ask questions in plain English.

Where Smartsheet shines

  • Back-office tracking: projects, vendors, budgets
  • Gantt charts and timelines for big jobs like repaving or a clubhouse renovation
  • Automations: alerts, approvals, and assignments
  • Dashboards and reports for board meetings
  • Free seats for viewers; anyone with the link can submit a form

If your board already runs its projects in Smartsheet, keep doing it. It's a strong tool.
